Installing the app

On Android Chrome, open the GitHub Pages website, then use Install app in Chrome's menu if it is offered. This app works offline after initial loading, but SMS and WhatsApp need connectivity and a compatible messaging app.

Customer messages are prepared on your device. The dealer must press Send in SMS or WhatsApp. The app logs when messaging is opened, but cannot verify delivery or that a message was sent.
